The Eastron SDM230 is the single-phase meter. In the standard kits it has one job: measuring the electricity of the pump at a water station. It is also the meter for a site whose whole supply is single-phase, where it takes the place of the three-phase SDM630. Greecon reads it by its own register, which is not the SDM630's, so it matters which of the two is fitted.

## At a glance

@@GLANCE@@

## How it qualifies

It is an approved model: the make and the model are named.

**It qualifies if:**

- {{cb}} It is an Eastron **SDM230** with Modbus (RS-485). The model on its label is the model on the quote.
- {{cb}} What it measures is single-phase: one pump, a small load, or a single-phase supply.
- {{cb}} The maker's manual came with it. Versions differ, and so does the speed a meter is delivered at: read the one in its box.

::: stop
**Fit the model the install card lists.** An SDM630 fitted where an SDM230 was planned is read as an SDM230 and shows one phase's power only. A three-phase pump takes the SDM630 ([sheet HW05](#hw-meter3)), not this meter.
:::

## At each kind of site

@@SITES@@

## What Greecon reads from it

One number: the **total active power**. It is input register 12 (counted from zero; the manual's 30013), a 32-bit floating-point number, high half first, in watts. Greecon shows it in kW. The template fills the register in: nobody types it.

| Fitted as | Reading in Greecon | How it is made from the power |
|-----------------|-----------------|------------------------------------|
| Pump meter (number 4), at a water station | **{{ui.reading_consumption}}**, kW | The size of the power: the pump's electricity |
| Grid meter (number 1), on a single-phase supply | **{{ui.reading_grid_import}}** and **{{ui.reading_grid_export}}**, kW | The power while it is positive, and while it is negative |
| Solar meter (number 2), on a single-phase plant | **{{ui.reading_solar}}**, kW | The size of the power |

## Settings, and where they are made

Read from the meter's own display, never assumed. Greecon sets them at the office; on site you only check them, and photograph the display.

| Setting | Value | Where |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------|----------------|
| Unit number | 4 as the pump meter (1 as a grid meter, 2 as a solar meter). It is delivered as 1 | On its display, at the office |
| Speed | **Set it to 9600 baud.** The speed it is delivered at depends on its version: the V1.6 manual says 2400 | On its display, on the Baud Rate page, at the office |
| Parity and stop bits | No parity, 1 stop bit | On its display, at the office |

A meter's display works only when it has power, and powering a meter on a bench is mains work. The electrician makes up the office's bench lead. The project manager never wires mains.

## Fitting

{{who:e}} On the feed of the pump (or where the single-phase supply enters), with the customer's lock-out on, as the meter's manual draws it. The project manager watches, photographs and writes it down.

- {{cb}} At a water station the meter sits in the board that feeds the pump, not in Greecon's enclosure.
- {{cb}} The electrician connects the data cable at the meter.
- {{cb}} **Photograph** the wiring, the label (model and serial number) and the display with its number and its speed.

## In the platform

- **Finding it.** In step 4 of the setup list, **{{step:4}}**, press **{{ui.find_equipment}}**. Found at number 4 as the SDM230 that was planned, its address is filled in and the line says **{{ui.found_set}}**.
- **Where an SDM630 was planned** (a hotel or a solar plant on a single-phase supply): the line says *It is a single-phase SDM230, not the three-phase SDM630 planned. If you use this address, Greecon reads it as a single-phase SDM230.* Press **{{ui.use_this_address}}** if that is what the site has. The change is written in the audit trail, and the line keeps saying which model Greecon reads.
- **The other way round.** If the line says *It also answers the three-phase meter's total-power register, so it may be a three-phase SDM630*, look at the label on the meter. If it is an SDM630, remove this device and add the three-phase meter from **{{ui.add_known_equipment}}**.
- **No pump meter at this site:** press **{{ui.site_does_not_have}}** under it.

## Acceptance test

**At the office:**

- {{cb}} The number and the speed (9600) are read from the meter's display, and photographed.
- {{cb}} On the desk, behind the converter, the box finds it at its number and reads its power:

```
greecon-edge find --host 192.168.1.50
greecon-edge modbus-test --host 192.168.1.50 --unit 4 --type input --address 12 --data-type float32
```

## What goes wrong

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
|-------------------|----------------------|------------------------------|
| **The meter does not answer**, and the modules on the same cable do | Its speed was left as delivered (2400, the V1.6 manual says) and not set to the cable's 9600; or its number was left at 1 | Read its speed and its number on its display against the install card. If they differ, the meter was not set at the office: take it back there, or fit the spare that was set there. Do not change it on site |
| **The reading is about a third of what the display shows** | The meter is a three-phase SDM630, read as an SDM230: one phase only | Check the label. Remove the device and add the three-phase meter from **{{ui.add_known_equipment}}** |
| **The reading is zero while the pump runs** | The meter is on the wrong feed | The electrician confirms which circuit it is on |

More is in {{ref:runbooks}}: R2 (a device does not answer), R3 (a reading looks wrong) and R16 (a device is found as another model).

## Not yet proven

- This software has not yet read a real SDM230. The register and the delivered speed are from the maker's manual (V1.6), and everything is tested against a pretend meter. The first real kit is rehearsed on a desk, and the first reading at a site is compared with the meter's own display.
- The prices are planning figures from shop listings seen in October 2026, not quotes.
