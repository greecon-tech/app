## Buying record {.newpage}

Ask three suppliers for a quote and keep the quotes in the shared drive. Buy one, pass the acceptance test with it, and only then buy more. For every part that is bought the hardware register keeps the make and model, the supplier, the date, the serial number or the batch, and the invoice.
