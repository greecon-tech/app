A water tank, a pump and the pipe it feeds. Greecon measures how full the tank is, the pressure in the pipe, whether the pump runs and, where the parts are fitted, the pump's flow and the electricity the pump uses. With the flow meter and the pump's running contact together, Greecon can say that the pump is running but no water is flowing. This list is every part of the kit for that, with the sheet that says how each one qualifies.

## The numbers on the data cable

@@UNITS@@

Everything on the cable is set to 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit, and the converter to port 502, in Modbus TCP to RTU gateway mode. Every number is set at the office, before the kit leaves.

## What the survey decides

| Ask, or look | It decides |
|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| How tall is the tank inside, and does it hold drinking water? | The level transmitter's range, and what it may be made of (sheet HW10) |
| What pressure does the pipe see? | The pressure transmitter's range (sheet HW11) |
| Has the pump's starter a spare contact that follows the pump? | Whether the pump contact module is fitted (sheet HW09) |
| Does the owner want to be told when the pump runs and no water flows? | The flow meter, which needs the pump's contact as well (sheet HW12) |
| Does the owner want the pump's electricity measured, and is the pump single-phase or three-phase? | The pump meter: the SDM230 for a single-phase pump, the SDM630 for a three-phase one, or none (sheets HW06 and HW05) |
| What does the pump do when the tank is low and when it is full, and who is told today? | Nothing to buy. Greecon only reports: the float switches and trips already there stay the safety layer |
| Is there internet the box may use, where the enclosure will be? | The cellular router (sheet HW04) |
