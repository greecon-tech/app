A hotel or a guesthouse with a water tank and its pump, solar on the roof and a grid connection: the usual first site. Greecon measures what is bought from the grid and what is sent to it, what the solar plant produces, how full the tank is, the pressure in the pipe and, where the parts are fitted, the pump's flow and whether the pump runs. This list is every part of the kit for that, with the sheet that says how each one qualifies.

## The numbers on the data cable

@@UNITS@@

Everything on the cable is set to 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit, and the converter to port 502, in Modbus TCP to RTU gateway mode. Every number is set at the office, before the kit leaves.

## What the survey decides

| Ask, or look | It decides |
|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Is the supply three-phase, and how many amps a phase? | The meters. Up to 100 A a phase: the SDM630 MCT, with clamps. Above that: the SDM630 with 5 A current transformers. A single-phase supply: the SDM230 (sheets HW05 and HW06) |
| How tall is the tank inside, and does it hold drinking water? | The level transmitter's range, and what it may be made of (sheet HW10) |
| What pressure does the pipe see? | The pressure transmitter's range (sheet HW11) |
| Has the pump's starter a spare contact that follows the pump? | Whether the pump contact module is fitted (sheet HW09) |
| Does the owner want to be told when the pump runs and no water flows? | The flow meter, which needs the pump's contact as well (sheet HW12) |
| Is there internet the box may use, where the enclosure will be? | The cellular router (sheet HW04) |
| Where does the enclosure go, and is the place dry? | IP54 or IP65 (sheet HW17) |

The whole job at this kind of site, from the survey to the handover, is in the site pack for a hotel or guesthouse (PDF 04).
