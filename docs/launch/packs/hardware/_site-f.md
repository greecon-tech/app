A farm or a greenhouse with irrigation: the soil and the air at the field, with the water supply behind it. Greecon measures the moisture of the soil in each irrigation zone, the air temperature and humidity, how full the tank is, the pressure in the irrigation line and, if there is a pump to watch, the pump's flow and whether it runs. This list is every part of the kit for that, with the sheet that says how each one qualifies.

## The numbers on the data cable

@@UNITS@@

A second soil probe, for a second zone, takes the next free number: 8, then 9. The numbers of the probes and of the weather sensor are set **before the probe is buried and the sensor is mounted.** Everything on the cable is set to 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit, and the converter to port 502, in Modbus TCP to RTU gateway mode.

## What the survey decides

| Ask, or look | It decides |
|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| How many irrigation zones are there? | How many soil probes (sheet HW13) |
| Which crop, which soil, how deep are the roots, and which spot is typical of the zone? | Where each probe goes and how deep. The irrigation limit is the grower's own, and is asked after the first week |
| At what air temperature does frost damage the crop, and in which weeks? | The frost limit, and who is woken (sheet HW14) |
| How tall is the tank inside, and what pressure does the line see? | The ranges of the level and pressure transmitters (sheets HW10 and HW11) |
| Is there a pump to watch, and has its starter a spare contact that follows it? | The pump contact module and the flow meter (sheets HW09 and HW12) |
| Is there a phone signal at the pump house, or a network cable to it? | The cellular router, which is the likelier case in a field (sheet HW04) |
| How far is it from the pump house to the farthest probe? | The data cable: plan the whole run under 100 metres (sheet HW03) |
| Does this site get a UPS? | The standard list for this kit has none. Decide, and write the decision down (sheet HW16) |
