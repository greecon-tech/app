The platform keeps its own list for each site. Open the site's setup page (**{{ui.set_up_site}}** and the site's name). It shows up to ten steps, always in this order; a step that does not apply to the site is left out. Each step is **Done**, **To do**, **Waiting** for another step, or **Needs attention**, and the banner at the top, **{{ui.do_next}}**, names the step to do next. The platform works most steps out by itself when it sees them work: you press buttons only for what a person must check. A step opens to show its detail and stays as you leave it.
