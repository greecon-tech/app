**Six rules for the whole job.**

1. **Greecon only reads.** It measures; it never switches anything. Never connect a wire from Greecon's equipment to a pump starter, an inverter's control, a valve or any safety circuit.
2. **Anything inside a distribution board is the electrician's work,** under the customer's lock-out procedure, with the project manager watching and writing it down. Nobody works on a live conductor.
3. **The project manager does the low-voltage and software side only:** the box, the network, the converter, the data cable between meters, the 4 to 20 mA signal wires, and the 12 or 24 V supply from a plug-in supply.
4. **Never touch the solar panels' wiring (DC), a tank you must climb into, wastewater or chemicals.** The customer's own staff or installer does that. If the only place for a sensor is there, say it is not possible and write why.
5. **Greecon is not a safety system.** The protection already in the customer's equipment (float switches, relief valves, trips) stays the safety layer.
6. **Write everything down at the time.** Photograph every meter's label and display, the wiring before the board is closed, the converter's settings and the box's label.
