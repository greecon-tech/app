Commissioning is checking, once, on site, that what Greecon shows is what the real world is. {{who:pm}} with the electrician and the owner's own person.

- Compare in the order of the table, and write the pair of numbers (what Greecon shows, what you can see) on the record.
- **Do not start, stop or open any equipment yourself.** The owner's own person does, because they know what else it affects.
- A check that fails is not commissioned: find the cause (the three most likely problems are at the end of this pack), fix it and check again. When every line passes, press **{{ui.commission_this_site}}** on the setup list (step 10) with a note. The progress line then reads **Commissioned.**, and your name and the date are on the handover sheet.
