There is one pack for each kind of site Greecon is sold for. A pack is complete on its own: it says what the site suits and measures, what to buy, what to ask at the survey, how everything is wired and connected (a system, a wiring, a network and an enclosure drawing for that kit), what to do at the office and on install day step by step with a box to tick and a name against every step, the addresses and registers to write down, the platform's ten setup steps in order, the commissioning tests with room for the numbers, the handover record, and the three problems most likely to meet you.

The four packs follow the same chapters in the same order, so that you find the same thing in the same place at every site. What they have in common is the same everywhere: the unit numbers (grid 1, solar 2, water module 3, optional load or pump meter 4), the data cable at 9600 baud with 8 data bits, no parity and 1 stop bit, the converter on port 502 in gateway mode, and Greecon only reading. The reasons for each part, its settings and the mistakes to avoid are in the Hardware Reference (Part 4).

A site that fits none of the four is not forced into one. The platform has an empty template (nothing assumed); use it only when none of the four fits, and ask the founder first.
